# RQ2 expressed post-decision expectation

The primary comparison was P(EXP_UNCLEAR | Middle) versus P(EXP_UNCLEAR | Extreme), where Middle=ranks 6–9 and Extreme=High+Low (ranks 1–5 or 10–14). The trial-level rates were 0.0357 for Middle and 0.0195 for Extreme. The participant-level paired difference (Extreme−Middle) was -0.0192, bootstrap 95% CI [-0.0350, -0.0048], n=98 participants.

These labels describe expressed post-decision expectation language only; they are not true beliefs, internal expectation, latent cognition, or a causal mediator. Coding agreement was computed before adjudication and is reported in `LLM_CODER_AGREEMENT.csv`.
